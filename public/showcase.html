04 / Research direction

The animal, its environment, and what happens next.

The long-term goal is to understand relationships between the individual animal, its changing environment, biological state and response to intervention—not merely the sensors observing them. The first credible experiment is smaller: group-level observations alongside timestamped water tests and keeper actions.

Question

Does fusion add value?

Compare synchronized observations against periodic water testing alone.

Method

Small, supervised pilot

Begin with group-level fish-zone behaviour, calibration, timestamps, confounders and missing-data handling.

Boundary

No automated care claims

No validated diagnosis, individual-animal welfare score or autonomous intervention.
