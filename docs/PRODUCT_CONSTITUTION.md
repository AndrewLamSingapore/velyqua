# VELYQUA Product Constitution

**Status:** Authoritative product direction v2.0

**Effective date:** 12 August 2026

**Launch focus:** iPhone-first, English, Singapore, freshwater only

**Working name:** VELYQUA, pending formal legal and App Store clearance

## Product thesis

VELYQUA is a personal freshwater tank agent and human-updated digital twin. It asks for seconds, returns one justified decision and learns from the real outcome.

It is not a generic chatbot, social feed, autonomous controller, animal identity system or marketplace.

## Mission

Give freshwater aquarium owners back their time while helping them make calmer, safer and better-informed care decisions.

## Launch owner

The primary owner is an English-speaking adult in Singapore with one to three established tropical freshwater aquariums. They already perform basic care, use an iPhone beside the tank, sometimes forget the last intervention and value confidence more than collecting charts.

Initial supported profiles are:

- freshwater community;
- planted low-tech;
- planted with injected CO2, as a limited research subgroup;
- shrimp, where reviewed content coverage exists; and
- large or exotic freshwater, as a discovery cohort rather than a default launch claim.

## Product promise

Tell it once. See what matters. Try before you act.

The owner records tests, observations and care manually through taps, confirmed speech drafts and photographs. VELYQUA maintains a transparent Tank Memory, returns one action or a confident no-action result, previews bounded changes and follows up to compare the expected direction with the real outcome.

Success means the owner spends less time managing aquarium information and more time enjoying a healthy tank.

## Core loop

1. **Quick Update** records a confirmed fact or action in seconds.
2. **Tank Memory** separates facts, estimates and unknowns.
3. **Aqua Now** returns exactly one current state and at most one primary action.
4. **Try a Change** compares a no-action baseline with bounded alternatives without changing real history.
5. **Quiet Plan** fits useful care to the time the owner has.
6. **Outcome Check** records what happened and closes the loop.

## Governing principles

1. **Protect attention.** Every prompt must be justified by a decision it can change.
2. **One action.** VELYQUA prioritizes; it does not transfer a backlog to the owner.
3. **No action is valid.** “Nothing needs attention” is a successful result.
4. **Fact before inference.** Raw owner records remain visible and are never silently replaced by estimates.
5. **Unknown before false comfort.** Missing or stale information may produce `More information needed`, never an invented all-clear.
6. **Smallest useful intervention.** Prefer measuring, waiting or the smallest safe change over stacked intervention.
7. **Outcome closes the loop.** Guidance is incomplete until the owner can record the actual result.
8. **Regional facts are governed.** Trade names, legal links and care content require sources, reviewers, versions and dates.
9. **AI is bounded.** AI may draft or explain. Deterministic rules and verified records decide product state.
10. **Owner control.** The owner confirms consequential drafts, corrects records, exports data and deletes the account.
11. **No commercial distortion.** Care advice is independent of advertising, affiliate placement and retailer funding.
12. **Calm safety.** Urgency is rare, specific, evidenced and paired with an appropriate next step.

## Product language

| Concept | VELYQUA term | Contract |
|---|---|---|
| Home | Aqua Now | One state, one primary action, why it matters and how long it should take |
| Capture | Quick Update | Common logs without multi-screen navigation |
| History | Tank Memory | Facts, estimates, unknowns, trends and chronological records |
| Simulation | Try a Change | Immutable snapshot, no-action baseline and bounded scenarios |
| Planning | Quiet Plan | Useful work fitted to the owner’s time budget |
| Personal baseline | Tank Normal | A learned descriptive band, never a replacement for species constraints |
| Regional content | Freshwater Library | Source-linked taxa, aliases, context, legal links and review status |
| Workload | Care Load | A calm summary of useful work, never a judgment of the owner |

## Decision contract

Every authoritative recommendation must:

- use deterministic, versioned rules;
- distinguish recorded facts, owner observations, derived state and estimates;
- show the triggering evidence, rule revision, freshness, confidence and limitations;
- ask for one decision-changing measurement when evidence is insufficient;
- support a valid wait-and-observe or no-action result;
- preserve the exact input snapshot needed for later replay; and
- support immediate rule withdrawal without rewriting historical records.

Generated language may explain an approved result. It may not invent measurements, species identity, safety rules, diagnoses or biological certainty.

## Manual-first and sensor-ready

The complete MVP works without sensors. Manual entry is the authoritative launch path.

The event model may accept validated sensor or controller readings later, but no hardware integration is required or promised in the MVP. VELYQUA never performs automatic physical control of heaters, feeders, pumps, lights, dosers or other equipment.

## Hard boundaries

The following are outside this product baseline:

- marine, reef, saltwater, pond and commercial-aquaculture management;
- Fish Passports, QR animal identity, ownership history, ownership transfer or livestock transaction records;
- a live-animal marketplace, valuation or assurance that a species is legal to own or trade;
- disease diagnosis, medication selection, prescription or drug dosing;
- automatic equipment control;
- photograph-only diagnosis or photographs presented as laboratory measurements;
- a public social feed, engagement streaks, shaming or low-value notifications;
- advertising, affiliate bias or retailer-funded care recommendations; and
- copied competitor code, copy, data, prompts, rules, interfaces, screenshots or private APIs.

Later support for Android, owner-facing web, caretakers, camera-assisted test reading, optional device ingestion and professional operations requires evidence and an approved scope change. It is not part of the launch promise.

## Data and trust contract

- The owner controls correction, export and deletion.
- A confirmed update is saved locally before network completion.
- Offline updates remain visible, retry safely and must not duplicate.
- Tank records and media are private by default and protected by owner-scoped authorization.
- No service-role or private API key enters the mobile bundle.
- Real owner photographs or records are never used as demo data without explicit, revocable permission.
- Every rule and knowledge record is versioned, reviewable, correctable and withdrawable.
- Missing evidence lowers confidence; it never creates reassuring fiction.

## Commercial posture

The founding beta is for product validation. Care quality and animal-welfare protections are never degraded to create fear, advertising inventory or payment pressure.

Commercial terms, vendor negotiations and unapproved pricing experiments are not part of this public product constitution.

## Governing rule

Build the smallest system that lets a busy owner update the tank in seconds, understand what matters, choose one useful action and safely test a change. Everything else waits for evidence.
