# Research Intelligence Decision

**Effective:** 26 August 2026
**Status:** Approved repository architecture; execution remains evidence-gated

## Decision

VELYQUA will maintain a separated scientific-research radar and commercial-intelligence radar connected through a canonical opportunity registry.

The architecture distinguishes:

`scientifically reported → commercially claimed/observed → VELYQUA hypothesis → planned → instrumented → collecting → experimentally supported → validated → productized`

These are not automatic transitions. External evidence may motivate an experiment but cannot validate VELYQUA.

## Initial radars

- Fishes/MDPI scientific landscape;
- OceanStar adjacent commercial intelligence; and
- a unified opportunity and market-gap matrix.

OceanStar is classified as an adjacent industrial-RAS signal, not a verified direct competitor, until product, customer, price, deployment and independent evidence meet the promotion gate.

## Product and safety impact

- No change to Prototype V0 sensor set or proof question.
- No expansion of disease-diagnosis scope.
- No autonomous equipment control.
- No copying of competitor expression or proprietary information.
- No claim that a scientific paper or vendor system validates VELYQUA.
- No large-scale ingestion infrastructure before the first real VELYQUA telemetry evidence.

## Delivery impact

The research layer is documentation and machine-readable registry data. It does not change the current application, capability states, release requirements or user-facing product.

## Withdrawal path

Individual sources may be corrected, retracted, expired, superseded or rejected without deleting their record. Opportunities may be narrowed or rejected. If the research layer creates scope drift or unsupported claims, freeze ingestion and revert decisions to the last evidence-backed state.

## Authority

The repository SSOT and Product Constitution remain higher authority. The Ecosystem Reality Map governs what is actually implemented or validated. The research directory cannot silently change normative product scope.
