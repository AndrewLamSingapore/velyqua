**Status:** Adjacent commercial signal; not independently verified as a direct competitor

## Classification

OceanStar Technologies publicly positions a smart aquaculture platform for commercial recirculating aquaculture systems (RAS). Its public pages describe industrial sensors, an ESP32-based hub, cloud analytics, cross-parameter machine learning, alerts, reporting and relay-based control.

Those pages are vendor-controlled sources. This radar records the claims and their implications; it does not certify their accuracy, deployment scale, model performance, customer outcomes or economic results.

## What OceanStar changes

OceanStar is evidence that the broad language of continuous monitoring, parameter interaction, early warning and ESP32-based edge acquisition is not unique. VELYQUA cannot rely on those phrases as a moat.

VELYQUA's potential differentiation is narrower and harder:

1. a fair comparison with periodic testing alone;
2. explicit false alarms, misses, lead time and calibration;
3. fusion of manual/reference chemistry with cheap continuous signals;
4. decision-specific instrumentation qualification;
5. owner-time protection, abstention and smallest-useful verification;
6. action/outcome memory; and
7. a consumer-safe boundary that does not confuse inference with permission to actuate.

## Unknowns required before competitor promotion

- verified customer segments and deployments;
- independently confirmed sensor/model performance;
- pricing, installation and lifecycle cost;
- data ownership, export and API terms;
- calibration, replacement and maintenance burden;
- false-alarm, miss and model-calibration evidence;
- exact alert-to-action workflow and human override behavior;
- customer references or third-party validation; and
- relevance to home/ornamental freshwater users.

Until those facts exist, OceanStar remains **adjacent commercial intelligence**, not a verified direct competitor or benchmark.
